Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Who we are

Rollout Data builds expert training data and runs AI services work from Bengaluru, India. For any question about your data, write to nishant@rolloutdata.com.

Signing in to our internal tools

Our team and invited partners sign in to our private tools with Google. When you do, we receive your name and email address from Google and use them only to check that you are on our access list. We do not receive your Google password, contacts, mail or files.

We keep a log of activity in these tools, including who searched for what, for 90 days, then delete it.

Business contact information

To find people to work with, we keep business contact information about professionals: name, role, employer, city, public profile links and work email or phone where available. It comes from public sources, data providers and our own records. We use it only to decide whom to contact about work. We do not sell it.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare, which hosts our tools and handles sign-in.
  • Google, for sign-in.
  • Anthropic, whose AI model answers questions in our tools. It receives only the short excerpts a question needs and does not train on them.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email nishant@rolloutdata.com and we will reply within 30 days.

This website sets no cookies.
